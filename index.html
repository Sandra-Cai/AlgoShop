Email Access

We scan your email newsletter subscriptions (e.g. Nike, Sephora, Amazon) to detect brands you follow. This helps us surface personalized deals and price drops for products you care about. We only read subscription metadata — never personal emails.

Phone Contacts

We import your phone contacts to connect you with friends already on AlgoShop. See what your friends are buying, share strategies, and discover deals through your social circle. Contact data is encrypted and never shared with third parties.

Your Privacy

Your data is encrypted end-to-end. We never sell, share, or monetize your personal information. You can revoke access to email or contacts at any time in Settings. All data processing follows GDPR and CCPA guidelines.
